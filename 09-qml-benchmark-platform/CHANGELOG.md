# Changelog

## [0.2.0] - 2026-08-29

### Added

- Sprint 2 Quantum Kernel Results Visualizer.
- Fair, bounded evaluation protocol and typed kernel/model registries.
- Four kernel matrices with sample-order, symmetry and checksum invariants.
- Paired QSVM, VQC, logistic and RBF-SVM runs with metrics and resource evidence.
- Idempotent P50/P51 evidence imports and JSON/CSV/HTML comparison reports.
- Data-dense Dash heatmaps, accessible tables, uncertainty and validity panels.
- Always-run PostgreSQL migration service and Sprint 2 cross-layer gate.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-26

### Added

- Sprint 1 Quantum Data Encoding Visualizer.
- Versioned, leakage-safe dataset snapshot pipeline.
- Basis, angle and amplitude encoders with Qiskit and PennyLane evidence.
- FastAPI, PostgreSQL and HTTP-only Dash architecture.
- Evidence manifests, contracts, tests and reproducible Docker quality gate.
